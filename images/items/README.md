# Item photos

Put photos for Collection and Gallery items here.

- Name each photo by its item code: `BS-001.jpg`, `AM-004.jpg`, `W-002.jpg`.
- Before uploading, resize to about 1200px on the longest side (aim for under 300 KB).
  Free tools: squoosh.app in the browser, or your phone's "medium" export size.
- Square or portrait photos on a plain background look best in the grid.
- In `data/items.json`, the image field is then `images/items/BS-001.jpg`.
