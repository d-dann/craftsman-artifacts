Common questions

A few quick answers. For anything else, just message us.

Do I need to be Buddhist?

No. Our practice comes from Buddhist tradition, and we welcome everyone, whatever their faith or background.

How much does it cost?

Every situation is different, so we'd like to understand yours first. Message us and we'll talk it through.

Are the pieces consecrated?

Yes. Our Master consecrates each piece, brings it to your home, helps place it in a suitable Feng Shui spot, and consecrates it again there.

Can I collect a piece instead?

Yes. We'll arrange pick-up or delivery with you after we chat.
